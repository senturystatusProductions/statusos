# StatusOS v5.0.0 - Business Intelligence Foundation

## Added
- A unified local Business Knowledge Graph connecting artists, projects, tasks, activities, and invoices.
- Deterministic opportunity scoring for artists and projects.
- A Business Intelligence panel inside Command Center.
- Recommended Next Move based on current StatusOS data.
- Top Opportunity ranking.
- Business snapshot metrics for open tasks, active projects, monthly revenue, and outstanding invoices.
- Business Intelligence context is now included automatically in AI requests.

## Notes
- No Supabase migration is required.
- Existing StatusOS data remains compatible.
- The graph is rebuilt locally from current data and does not replace the original records.
